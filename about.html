Our Heritage

Revolutionizing Two-Wheeler Buying & Selling

SMR Motors was founded on a simple realization: buying or selling a pre-owned motorcycle was often plagued by opaque pricing, hidden mechanical faults, and complex paperwork.

We dismantled those barriers. Every motorbike in our inventory undergoes a rigorous 45-point mechanical inspection by master technicians. We verify structural frame integrity, engine compression, electrical harnesses, and legal paperwork before any motorcycle is offered for sale.

Certified Quality

The 45-Point Certification Process

No compromises. Every bike is tested for performance, reliability, and safety.

Engine & Transmission

Oil health, spark ignition, compression testing, smooth clutch disengagement, and quiet gear mesh.

Brakes, Tyres & Suspension

Disc rotor thickness, caliper seals, ABS sensor diagnostics, tread depth, and front fork damping.

Electricals & ECU

Battery load check, alternator charging rate, headlights, LED blinkers, and onboard digital diagnostics.

Chassis & Legal Vetting

Laser-measured frame alignment, no flood/accident history, authentic chassis & engine stamping match.
