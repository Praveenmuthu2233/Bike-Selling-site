Buyer Advisory & Marketplace Role

Platform Disclaimer & Notice

Please read this vehicle marketplace advisory carefully before submitting an offer, purchasing a motorcycle, or conducting transactions on SMR Motors.

Applies to all listings across Tamil Nadu & India • Last Revised: November 2026

1. Marketplace & Intermediary Capacity

SMR Motors acts as a digital marketplace, showcase platform, and dealership intermediary connecting independent motorcycle sellers with prospective buyers. Where indicated, vehicles listed by private sellers represent peer-to-peer inventory where SMR Motors facilitates discovery, showroom staging, and preliminary inspection.

2. Seller-Provided Specifications & Verification

Motorcycle specifications—including manufacturing year, odometer reading (kilometers driven), engine displacement (CC), modifications, service history, and condition remarks—are provided directly by the seller or preliminary owner records.

Buyer Advisory: All prospective buyers must independently inspect and verify vehicle specifications, engine condition, chassis numbers, and documentation before entering into a binding financial agreement or making payments.

3. Physical Vehicle Inspection & Test Rides

Buyers are strongly encouraged to inspect motorcycles in person at our authorized showroom or designated inspection center in Salem. You are welcome to bring a certified mechanic or trusted technician to examine mechanical and electrical components. No guarantee or warranty of mechanical fitness should be implied without a formalized, documented dealership inspection certificate.

4. Documentation, RC Transfer & Clear Title

Prior to completing any vehicle purchase, buyers and sellers are jointly responsible for ensuring the following documents are legitimate and unencumbered:

  • Original Registration Certificate (RC Smart Card).
  • Valid Insurance Policy (Third-Party or Comprehensive).
  • Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate.
  • RTO Transfer Forms (Form 29 and Form 30) signed by the registered owner.
  • Hypothecation Termination (Form 35 and NOC from financier, if previously financed).

5. Pricing Variations & Inventory Availability

Listed prices are subject to negotiation, market fluctuations, and seller counteroffers. An online listing does not constitute an irrevocable commercial offer. A motorcycle is only reserved when a formal booking deposit is acknowledged in writing by dealership staff. Availability is subject to prior sale.

6. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted under applicable consumer protection legislation and Indian contract law, SMR Motors disclaims liability for inaccuracies in seller-supplied listing details, undisclosed pre-existing mechanical defects, or off-platform disputes. Users must conduct transactions through verified dealership communication channels.
