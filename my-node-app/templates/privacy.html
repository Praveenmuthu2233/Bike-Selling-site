Privacy Policy & Data Rights

At SMR Motors, we prioritize the privacy, security, and integrity of your personal information. This policy transparently outlines how data is collected, processed, and safeguarded.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

Exercise Your Privacy Rights

Request a full export of your stored personal data or initiate an account deletion request.

01. Introduction

Welcome to SMR Motors ("we," "our," or "the Platform"). SMR Motors operates a pre-owned motorcycle marketplace and dealership platform headquartered in Salem, Tamil Nadu, India.

This Privacy Policy explains how SMR Motors collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you visit our website (https://bike-selling-site-lco8.onrender.com) or use our services to browse, list, inspect, or purchase motorcycles. By using the Platform, you acknowledge the terms described herein.

02. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to deliver transparent motorcycle matchmaking, verify vehicle ownership, and enable communication between buyers, sellers, and dealership staff.

03. Personal Information

When you register, submit an inquiry, list a motorcycle, or contact us, we collect:

  • Full Name: Used for account personalization and official inquiry routing.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier, for booking notifications, and for sending counteroffers.
  • Phone Number: Used solely to coordinate vehicle inspections, test rides, and transfer paperwork.
  • Location / City: Used to display dealership location or vehicle handover point.

04. Google Account Authentication

We utilize Google Identity Services (Google OAuth 2.0) for seamless, passwordless user authentication. When you sign in with Google:

  • We receive your cryptographic Google ID token, verified through Google’s public key infrastructure.
  • We access only your verified name, primary email address, profile avatar URL, and Google user ID.
  • We never receive, request, or store your Google account password.
  • We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail messages, contacts, or calendar.

05. Bike Listing Information

When you submit a motorcycle for sale, we collect technical details including make, model, registration year, odometer kilometers, fuel type, transmission, vehicle registration number, seller requested payout, and condition description.

Before public publication, vehicle registration numbers are reviewed by our administration team. Private financial margins (such as confidential dealership expenses) are never disclosed to third parties.

06. Customer Enquiries

When buyers submit price proposals or test ride queries through our motorcycle details page, we record the message text, contact details, and timestamp in our enquiries ledger. Sellers and dealership staff use this information solely to review and respond to the specific query.

07. Uploaded Images

Users and dealership managers can upload up to 6 gallery photographs per motorcycle. Uploaded images are validated for size (under 5MB each) and sanitized to strip potentially dangerous embedded scripts. Images are stored securely on our application file storage and cataloged in our database.

08. How We Use Information

  • Publishing verified pre-owned listings to the live marketplace.
  • Facilitating buyer inquiries and seller counteroffers.
  • Dispatching real-time notifications when a listing is approved, rejected, or receives an offer.
  • Preventing fraudulent vehicle listings, spam, and platform abuse.
  • Complying with applicable motor vehicle documentation standards in Tamil Nadu.

09. Database Storage and Security

Our primary data layer is hosted on a managed TiDB Cloud MySQL-compatible distributed cluster.

  • Transport Security: All connections to our database gateway require mutual TLS verification using the root ISRG Certificate Authority (isrgrootx1.pem).
  • Encryption in Transit: HTTPS SSL/TLS encryption across all public web traffic.
  • Access Control: Administrative and staff endpoints enforce role-based access control (RBAC). Passwords for staff accounts are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2/SHA256 before storage.

10. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following trusted third-party providers:

  • Google Identity Services: Provides cryptographic sign-in and token validation.
  • TiDB Cloud (PingCAP): Enterprise cloud database infrastructure with TLS encryption.
  • Cloudflare / FontAwesome / Google Fonts: Public content delivery networks for styling and web fonts.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

11. Cookies and Sessions

The Platform uses JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored in browser localStorage or sessionStorage to maintain your authenticated session. We do not deploy invasive third-party tracking pixels or behavioral advertising cookies.

12. Data Retention

We retain active user accounts and motorcycle listings for as long as your account remains active. Completed sale transactions are archived in our sales ledger for financial compliance and accounting purposes as required by Indian commercial law.

13. User Rights

Under applicable digital data protection standards, you have the right to:

  • Access and review all personal data associated with your profile.
  • Correct inaccurate phone numbers, location, or vehicle specifications.
  • Withdraw your motorcycle listing at any time prior to finalized sale.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your profile and data records.

14. Account Deletion Requests

You can request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. Submit your request using the form below or email us directly at praveenmuthu9500@gmail.com. Upon identity confirmation, non-archived user records will be permanently expunged within 14 business days.

05. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended strictly for individuals aged 18 and older who hold legal capacity to operate and transfer registered motor vehicles under Indian law. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors.

16. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications in our features, security practices, or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be published here with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
